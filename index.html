01 — THE COURSE

Technology starts
with understanding.

Fundamentals of Computing introduces the core concepts that make modern technology possible.

Throughout the course, students explore computer hardware and software, programming, operating systems, computer networks, and databases. These topics help us understand how computers work and how technology supports everyday life.
