About the Course

Fundamentals of Computing introduces students to basic computer science concepts. Students learn about computer hardware, software, programming, operating systems, networks, and databases. These skills help students understand how computers work and how technology is used in everyday life.

Our Team Mission

Our mission is to create an informative and user-friendly website that showcases what we have learned throughout the Fundamentals of Computing course. We aim to share our knowledge, projects, videos, and experiences while developing our teamwork and technical skills.
